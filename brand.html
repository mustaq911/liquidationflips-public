The mark

Two shapes, one story. The white letterform anchors the brand while the green arrow drives it forward — overstock and returns turned into a deal, fast.

Primary mark
The default. Rounded navy tile, always.
On light surfaces
The tile keeps its contrast on white and grey.
Monochrome
One-colour ink for stamps, invoices, faxable docs.

Do

  • Keep clearspace of at least half the tile's height around the mark.
  • Use the navy tile on photography and busy backgrounds.
  • Scale the SVG — it stays crisp at any size.

Don't

  • Recolour the arrow or letterform off-palette.
  • Stretch, rotate or add shadows to the mark.
  • Place the white-cut mark on a light background.

Typography

One typeface, used with range. Hanken Grotesk — heavy for headlines, regular for body. Tight tracking on large sizes.
