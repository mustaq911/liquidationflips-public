How bidding works

Set your maximum bid

Enter the most you're willing to pay. We bid on your behalf up to that amount, one increment at a time — so you never pay more than you need to in order to stay in front.

Win when the timer ends

If you're the highest bidder when the auction closes, the lot is yours. You'll see the result on your All Bids & Results page and can pay from there.

Prefer not to wait?

Many lots are also available at a fixed Buy Now price — add to cart and check out instantly, no bidding required.

Fees & payment

No buyer's premium

There is currently no buyer's premium on Liquidation Flips — your winning bid (or Buy Now price) is the price, plus applicable taxes.

Secure card checkout

Pay securely by card at checkout — your winnings and Buy Now orders are processed through our encrypted payment provider.

New registrants may be asked to use an alternate payment method for their first invoice as part of standard card-security procedures.

Taxes

Applicable Canadian sales taxes are calculated and shown at checkout before you confirm payment.

Pickup & removal

Pick up your lots from our warehouse locations and inspect them on-site before you leave — no surprises.
